## Summary (≤ 300)
```text
Free script writer for podcasts, interviews, Reels, Shorts and voiceovers. Timed scripts with word budgets, pause marks and scored hooks, show notes and rule-checked chapters, paste-ready blocks for free TTS (ElevenLabs, Gemini, Azure) and a repurpose pack. No voice cloning of real people.
```

## Description
```markdown
Give a topic, length, audience and language. You get a script that sounds right out loud, timed to the second, plus everything you need to publish it.

You get:
- a word budget per minute (English, Azerbaijani, Russian, Turkish and more) and the final word count
- 3 scored hook options, pause and stress marks, timed blocks with on-screen text
- podcast, interview, two-host dialogue and trailer formats; a guest brief from what you paste
- a plain-speech pass with a pronunciation guide
- show notes, title options, thumbnail text and chapters checked against YouTube and Spotify rules
- paste-ready blocks for ElevenLabs v3, Gemini (one or two voices) and Azure SSML, with each free tier's real terms
- a repurpose pack (clip scripts, post, newsletter) and a publish checklist

**Where it stops:** text only, so you record the audio or paste it into a TTS tool. It never clones or imitates a real person's voice, never invents quotes or statistics, and adds an AI-voice disclosure line.
```

## Limitations / FAQ
- Free-tier terms were checked on 2026-11-11 and change often. ElevenLabs Free allows no commercial use and needs "elevenlabs.io" or "11.ai" in the title. Gemini free-tier text may be reviewed by humans, and users must be 18+.
- TikTok's AI-label rule couldn't be read on its help page, so the skill says "check in app".
- For deep Azerbaijani TTS clean-up (letters, Cyrillic, numbers), the owner's Azerbaijani Voiceover Studio skill goes further.
