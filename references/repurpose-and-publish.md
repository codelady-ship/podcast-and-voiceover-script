# Repurpose, transcript clean-up and publish checklist

## Repurpose pack (from one episode or long video)

| Piece | Length | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 3 clip scripts | 30–60 s each | Each starts with its own hook (rewrite only the first line; keep the speaker's words exact). Give the source timestamp (e.g. `from 12:40–13:30`). One idea per clip. |
| 1 carousel or post | 6–8 slides or 5–8 lines | The 3 takeaways + one CTA ("full episode: link in bio"). |
| 1 newsletter | subject + 5 lines | Subject under 50 characters; one link. |
| 1 LinkedIn-style post | 5–8 short lines | Lesson first, a question at the end, no hashtag list. |

Rules:
- Quotes are word for word from the script or transcript. Anything else is marked `[paraphrase]`.
- Using a guest's words in clips needs the guest's OK (part of the pre-interview consent).
- Never cut a clip so the guest seems to say something they did not mean.

## Transcript clean-up (pasted transcript)

Give an **edit list**, not a rewrite:
- `cut` filler: "um", "you know", repeated starts, silences over 2 s.
- `cut` retakes: keep the last good take and say which one.
- `check` unclear words: `[inaudible 14:05]`; never guess names or numbers.
- `fix` names and terms that auto-captions spelled wrong.

Format: `12:40–12:47 | cut | false start`.

## Publish checklist

- [ ] Title under 60 characters, topic first; thumbnail text of 4–5 words (video).
- [ ] Description: what the listener gets, 3 takeaways, credits, links.
- [ ] Chapters checked. YouTube: first `00:00`, at least 3, ascending, each at least 10 s. Spotify: first `00:00`, at least 3, in order, plain text without emoji, merge chapters shorter than 30 s.
- [ ] Sponsor or affiliate disclosed; the `[AD]` read clearly sounds like an ad.
- [ ] AI voice: disclosure line + platform label. ElevenLabs Free: "elevenlabs.io" or "11.ai" in the title, and no commercial use.
- [ ] Music licence noted (free: YouTube Audio Library or the platform's own sound library).
- [ ] Guest consent to publish; guest name spelled right.
- [ ] Loudness: Apple recommends about −16 LKFS (±1 dB) with true peak at or below −1 dBFS.
- [ ] Listen once on a phone speaker before publishing.
